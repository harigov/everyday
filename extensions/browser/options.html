Connect to Every Day

  1. In Every Day, open Settings → Screen time.
  2. Choose Connect a browser and copy the code.
  3. Paste it here and choose Connect.

What is sent

  • The site's address without the page (github.com, not the repository), the tab's title unless Every Day is set not to keep titles, when you started and stopped looking, and the browser's name.
  • Only to the address in the connection code: the app on this computer, or your own sharing server.

What is not

  • Full addresses, page contents, keystrokes, history or screenshots.
  • Tabs in windows that are not focused, or anything while you are idle or locked.
  • Private windows, browser pages and extension pages.
